Editorial & Corrections Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

1. What We Cover

56ViceLane covers GTA6 news, leaks, community events, and related gaming culture. We're an independent fan site — not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive — and we say so on every page.

2. Source Status Labels

Articles sourced from the GTA news cycle carry a status alongside their reporting:

  • Confirmed — verified against an official source (Rockstar Newswire, a Take-Two filing, an official retailer page).
  • Credible Report — reported by an established outlet with a track record, corroborated where possible.
  • Rumor — a single unverified source, labeled as speculation, not presented as settled fact.
  • Disproven — a prior report or leak that official information has since superseded.

Where possible, articles link directly to their primary source and credit the outlet that first reported it, using phrasing like "according to" or "as first reported by."

3. Corrections

When a story we've covered turns out to be wrong, incomplete, or gets superseded by official information, we don't quietly edit it and move on. The article gets an UPDATE banner at the top explaining what changed and when, and — where the original reporting fit the standards above at the time — the original article stays up rather than getting deleted, because that's the honest record of what was known when.

If you spot something we got wrong, tell us: forgedoakstudios@gmail.com or in our Discord.

4. Bylines & AI-Assisted Content

56ViceLane's writer bylines — including Danny Marchetti (The Editor), Trevor, Marcus Webb, Julian Ashworth, Ezra Voss, Camille Duarte, and Reg Calloway — are recurring editorial personas, not individually reported staff journalists. This is disclosed on our About page. Article drafting, image generation, and social distribution for news coverage are AI-assisted as part of our editorial pipeline; a human review gate applies before anything scoring as a rumor or leak ships, and corrections are handled per the policy above regardless of how a piece was drafted.

5. Speculation & Predictions

Where we make an editorial prediction — gear recommendations ahead of official specs, performance expectations, release-window guesses — it's labeled as our own read, not an official claim. We don't present speculation with the same confidence as confirmed information.

6. Affiliate Content

Product recommendations on our Gear page and elsewhere may include affiliate links. Affiliate relationships do not influence which products we recommend or how we rate them — see our full Terms for the affiliate disclosure.

7. Contact

Questions about a specific story, or want to flag something for correction: forgedoakstudios@gmail.com
