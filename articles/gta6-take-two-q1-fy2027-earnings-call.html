Take-Two Beats Q1, Raises FY2027 Guidance To $8–8.2B — And Confirms Netflix Is Involved

Take-Two posted $1.39B in Q1 net bookings, ahead of its own guidance, and raised its full-year outlook on the strength of GTA6 confidence. The headline for fans: a new extended look drops August 27 through a first-of-its-kind Netflix partnership.

August 7, 2026  •  56ViceLane Staff  •  Earnings · TTWO · GTA6

Take-Two Interactive held its first-quarter fiscal 2027 earnings call today, and CEO Strauss Zelnick opened by calling it an "excellent start" to the fiscal year. Net bookings came in at $1.39 billion, slightly above the high end of the company's own guidance, driven by NBA 2K and the Grand Theft Auto series. But the number that matters most to this audience is the one Take-Two raised on the back of it: full-year guidance now sits at $8–8.2 billion, and Zelnick was explicit that the increase reflects "high confidence" in GTA6 landing on November 19, 2026.

Take-Two's finance leadership also gave full-year targets beyond net bookings: GAAP net revenue guidance of $7.9–8.1 billion, cost of revenue of $3.54–3.66 billion, and second-quarter net bookings guidance of $1.62–1.67 billion (down from $1.96B in the prior-year quarter, a comparison the company attributed to timing rather than weakness). Mobile is expected to be down for the full year, as flagged going in — GTA, NBA 2K, and Zynga's mobile portfolio are the stated growth engines instead.

What Was Confirmed

✓ ConfirmedNovember 19, 2026 — Still The Date
Zelnick reaffirmed the release date is on track, and said confidence in that date directly informed the decision to raise full-year guidance. He also said pre-orders have had an "exceptional start" and anticipation will keep building through launch.
✓ ConfirmedExtended Look Coming August 27 — Via Netflix
Rockstar will release a new extended look at GTA6 on August 27, distributed through what Zelnick called a "groundbreaking," first-of-its-kind marketing and distribution partnership with Netflix — rather than solely through YouTube or Rockstar's own channels. He said Take-Two continues to work with essentially every major social platform on top of this, so expect it everywhere, not just on Netflix.
💰 FinancialFY2027 Guidance Raised To $8–8.2B, GTA6 Cited As The Reason
Zelnick tied the raise directly to GTA6 confidence, saying Take-Two expects to sustain a higher level of scale and generate strong cash flow for several years as the pipeline matures. Finance leadership added the Grand Theft Auto series, NBA 2K, and Zynga's mobile portfolio are expected to be the largest contributors to that full-year number, representing roughly 20% growth over fiscal 2026 at the midpoint.
✓ ConfirmedGTA Online's Court Center Heist Is A Deliberate Bridge To Launch
On July 14, Rockstar launched the Court Center Heist for GTA Online — a new heist targeting a Los Santos cultural institution, with artwork as the target. Zelnick said the update has been well received and is reactivating lapsed players ahead of GTA6, and framed it as part of a deliberate strategy to keep the GTA Online community engaged between now and November.

What Was NOT Announced

Not AnnouncedPrice
Asked directly about pricing strategy as other publishers move toward $80 price points, Zelnick declined to commit to a specific number for GTA6. He said Take-Two's philosophy is to deliver more value than it charges rather than maximize price, and pointed to GTA Online's sustained engagement 13 years after launch as evidence that approach works. Nothing about $70 vs. $80 vs. anything else was locked in on this call.
Not AnnouncedGTA6 Online Details
No specifics on GTA6 Online were discussed on this call. The focus stayed on GTA Online's current live service and its role as a reactivation bridge, not on what comes after launch.

Elsewhere On The Call

Most of the call's runtime went to the rest of Take-Two's portfolio rather than GTA6 specifically. NBA 2K just wrapped what Zelnick called an "outstanding" year, up 9%, with engagement metrics climbing across the board — average daily active users up 15%, MyCareer DAU up 25%. President Karl Slatoff confirmed NBA 2K27 will be officially unveiled on September 4, with a fuller preview coming August 18, and Caitlin Clark confirmed for the Deluxe Edition cover. On mobile, Toon Blast and Words With Friends both beat forecasts, and Borderlands 4 is getting more story content in September following its "Bounty Pack 4" DLC drop on July 30.

Asked about AI tooling inside Take-Two's studios, Zelnick said the company pursues both "basic" and "applied" AI research but framed it explicitly as a tool to enhance human creativity rather than replace it or cut costs — a contrast he drew deliberately against competitors publicly touting AI-driven savings.

Bottom Line

Nothing here changes the release date — November 19, 2026 is reaffirmed, and Take-Two just put its money where its mouth is by raising full-year guidance explicitly because of it. The fan-facing news is August 27: a new extended look, launching through Netflix in a partnership Rockstar has never done before. Price is still unconfirmed. Mark August 27, and expect more GTA Online content between now and launch.
