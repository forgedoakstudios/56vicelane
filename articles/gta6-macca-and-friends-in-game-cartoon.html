GTA6 Is Getting Its Own In-Game Cartoon, "Macca & Friends"

📅 November 16, 2026 ✍️ Reg Calloway ⏱️ 6 min read

Three days before launch, Rockstar put out a teaser confirming GTA6 is shipping with its own in-world animated series — "Macca & Friends," airing on the in-game Weazel network starting November 19, the same day the game itself launches, as first reported by Dexerto. The show stars Macca, a gator, alongside a cast of other talking animals. That's a real production, not a background gag — and it's the kind of detail this franchise has used before to make its world feel lived-in rather than just loud.

📼 A Real Show, Built For A TV That Doesn't Exist

GTA5's fake channels and in-universe ads became their own cult favorites over the better part of a decade precisely because they didn't have to exist — nobody would have noticed if Rockstar skipped them entirely. "Macca & Friends" fits that same lineage, except scaled up into what sounds like an actual produced series rather than a 30-second loop. According to Push Square, which cites a report from Kinda Funny, this isn't even the only in-world program in the works — multiple shows have reportedly been built for launch, including a telenovela and a separate animated series called "The Johnsons." If that holds up, GTA6's in-game TV isn't a joke shelf, it's a whole network's worth of content.

What nobody agrees on yet is the actual tone. Dexerto's framing leans into the Saturday-morning-cartoon angle — bright, silly, the kind of thing that would have run between cereal commercials in 1988. AllKeyShop describes it instead as a 2D animated series built in the Adult Swim mold, closer to adult-audience satire than kids' programming. Pasquale Pillitteri's coverage is more careful, noting the tone genuinely isn't clear from what Rockstar has shown so far. Given this franchise's history of using exactly that kind of "looks innocent, isn't" misdirection — the fake kids'-show aesthetic hiding something much more cynical underneath — both readings are plausible, and we won't know for sure until people are actually sitting in front of an in-game TV watching it.

📝 A Word From The Legacy Desk

I've watched this franchise build out its fictional media ecosystem since the radio stations were the whole trick — a handful of DJs and a tracklist, and that alone made the world feel like somewhere real people lived. Every generation since has added another layer: TV channels, fake ads, now apparently a full network's worth of original programming you can sit down and actually watch. That's not filler. That's the same instinct that made the radio stations work, just with thirteen more years of production budget behind it.

🤷 What's Still Unconfirmed

Rockstar's teaser hasn't specified an episode count, whether full episodes are watchable start to finish or looped in short segments, or exactly how players access the in-game network — through a specific TV, a menu, or just ambient background programming the way GTA5 handled its ads. Those are the practical questions that'll actually determine whether this is a genuine attraction or a nice-to-have background detail, and none of them are answered yet.

The Bottom Line

GTA6 is launching with at least one real in-world TV series and, if Kinda Funny's reporting holds up, possibly several — a level of built-out fictional media that goes well beyond what GTA5 shipped with at launch. Whether "Macca & Friends" plays it as straight kids' programming or Adult-Swim-style satire is still an open question three days out, and honestly, it being unclear which one it is might be the most GTA thing about it. Which read do you think is more likely — harmless Saturday-morning filler, or something a lot darker wearing a cartoon's face?
