GTA6 Won't Have First-Person Mode At Launch, Plus New Map Size Details

📅 August 28, 2026 ✍️ Julian Ashworth ⏱️ 4 min read

Two more technical details surfaced in the wake of the Extended Look: GTA6 is skipping a dedicated first-person mode at launch, and a new comparison lays out just how big the map actually is next to Rockstar's past open worlds.

👁️ No First-Person Mode At Launch

According to Insider Gaming, corroborated by TweakTown, Vice, and Beebom, Rockstar confirmed to creators who previewed the game at Rockstar North in Edinburgh that GTA6 won't ship with a dedicated first-person perspective option at launch. Players will still be able to aim certain weapons in first-person, but full free-roam first-person movement — the kind GTA5 eventually added — isn't part of the day-one release. Reporting suggests it reflects where Rockstar chose to focus its animation work: third-person movement and interactions, not a parallel first-person camera system.

🗺️ How Big Is The Map, Really?

Separately, per IGN, a new breakdown compares GTA6's map footprint to Red Dead Redemption 2 and GTA5 — both games long treated as the benchmark for Rockstar's open-world scale. The comparison doesn't land on one clean number (map-size math is notoriously inconsistent across outlets depending on how coastline, water, and interiors get counted), but the overall picture confirms GTA6's Leonida is a meaningful step up rather than a lateral move.

🎯 What This Means For You

If first-person exploration was a must-have for you, temper expectations for day one — it may come later, the way GTA5's did. If scale is what you're after, GTA6 is shaping up to deliver: a bigger world than either of Rockstar's previous open-world benchmarks.

📝 Editor's Note

We're keeping this at "reported" — the first-person detail comes from creators' accounts of a Rockstar-hosted preview, not a formal press release, and the map-size comparison is analysis built from available details rather than an official figure from Rockstar itself.

The Bottom Line

Neither detail changes the November 19 release date, but both add real texture to what launch day will actually look and feel like — third-person only, and bigger than anything Rockstar has built before.
