From The Editor's Desk: 9 Days Out

📅 November 10, 2026 ⏱️ 7 min read
Danny Marchetti
Editor-in-Chief

Nine days. Single digits start tomorrow and I have decided, as a coping mechanism, to treat "nine" as still basically two digits if you squint, which buys me exactly one more day of pretending this is a reasonable amount of time left. The second antacid drawer is now fully staffed. I bought a third bottle this morning not because the second ran out but because I wanted a spare, which is a sentence I never thought I'd write about stomach medicine, and yet here we are, nine days out, doing exactly that.

Marcus flagged me this morning — not a typo this time, an actual number, which is worse — and I want to walk you through what that did to me in real time, because I think it's relevant to the broader emotional arc we're all living through together right now.

💰 $3.5 Billion Is A Number I Am Choosing Not To Think About Today

Take-Two told investors on its Q2 earnings call that it's expecting as much as $3.5 billion during the GTA6 launch period, and that preorders are tracking "well above expectations" — CEO Strauss Zelnick's words, not mine, reportedly, according to PC Gamer and Eurogamer's coverage of the call. Zelnick says the game will "set a new baseline" for the company. I read that sentence four times. Then I stood up, walked to the window, looked at absolutely nothing out there for about ninety seconds, and sat back down. That's the whole update on how I processed it. I'm choosing to be proud of the number instead of afraid of it, which is working about sixty percent of the time, mostly in the mornings, before I've had coffee and the number has had a chance to fully load.

🗞️ What Else Actually Happened Today

Take-Two is also reportedly leaning harder into technical defenses and legal action against the AI-reverse-engineering crowd — the people trying to piece together a PC version from leaked files before there's an official one — which, fine, good, lock it down, I don't need one more variable in the next nine days where "someone built a fake early version and it leaked everywhere" becomes a headline I have to write about at 2 AM. There's also a report floating around that some preload data is missing region-by-region, which some outlets are reading as a possible censorship signal in certain markets — I'm treating that as unconfirmed, not alarming, filed under "things I will revisit if literally anyone official says a word about it."

😬 Editor's Note

I told Trevor about the $3.5 billion number specifically to see his face do something, and it did, and I regret showing him before coffee. He has started a notebook. I don't fully understand the notebook. I am choosing not to ask follow-up questions about the notebook, because I think the answers would require a fourth drawer, and I am setting a personal limit at three.

What We're Doing About It

Same as yesterday, same as the day before: daily Editor's Desk, daily Trevor's Take, straight through to the 19th. I've started doing a lap of the office between paragraphs now instead of just pacing at my desk, which means I'm getting actual steps in during deadline week, which my watch is thrilled about and nobody else is.

The bottom line

Nine days, a $3.5 billion forecast, a piracy crackdown, and a notebook I'm not allowed to ask about. Does a number that big make this feel more real to you, or does it just feel like a headline until you're actually holding the game in your hands?
