GTA6 Gameplay And Full Leonida Map Leak Online — Rockstar Fires Back With DMCA Takedowns

📅 August 18, 2026 ✍️ Ezra Voss ⏱️ 4 min read

Nine days before Rockstar's own carefully staged Netflix "Extended Look," someone appears to have gotten there first. A leaker going by "Cyberleek" began circulating GTA6 gameplay footage and what looks like the full Leonida map online — and Rockstar's response, a wave of DMCA takedowns, is doing about as much to confirm this is real as an official statement would.

🎥 What's Actually In The Leak

According to GamesRadar and corroborated by GameSpot, the leaked material includes what appears to be a near-final gameplay clip — roughly a minute long — showing Jason, one of GTA6's two playable protagonists, stepping out of an oceanside house and shooting hoops alone, plus what's being described as the full Leonida map. Multiple additional outlets, including Dexerto, Push Square, and NotebookCheck, are reporting the same story independently, which is exactly the kind of cross-source pattern that separates a real leak from a fabricated one doing the rounds on social media.

⚖️ Rockstar's Response Says More Than It Means To

Rockstar didn't confirm or deny anything on the record — companies almost never do with active leaks. But DMCA takedown notices started hitting accounts sharing the footage almost immediately, and that reaction is itself informative. You don't spend legal resources issuing copyright strikes against a fake. Whether or not Rockstar ever says the word "authentic" out loud, its lawyers already answered the question.

🎯 What This Means For You

Don't go hunting for the footage expecting it to still be up — DMCA takedowns move fast, and reposts are getting struck as they appear. More importantly: this doesn't change November 19. If anything, it adds pressure on Rockstar's own August 27 Netflix reveal to feel like a moment rather than old news, since a chunk of the internet may have already seen fragments of it by then.

👤 Who Is "Cyberleek"?

Unlike the 2022 leak, which came from an external hacking incident, this one is reportedly framed by the leaker themselves as a protest — specifically against Rockstar's decision to make GTA6 digital-only at launch, an issue we've covered before. Whether that framing holds up or is just cover for a more ordinary leak, we're treating the leaker's stated motive as reported claim, not verified fact, until there's more to go on.

📝 Editor's Note

We're calling this "reported," not "confirmed" — Rockstar hasn't issued an official statement acknowledging the footage's authenticity, and we can't independently verify the source video ourselves. But the DMCA response, the specificity of the details, and the number of separate trusted outlets converging on the same story independently is about as strong a signal as this kind of story gets before an official confirmation. We'll update if that changes.

The Bottom Line

Nothing here is officially confirmed by Rockstar, and won't be — studios don't comment on active leaks. But the DMCA response alone tells you what Rockstar believes internally, even if it never says so publicly. With the official Netflix reveal just nine days out, this is either spectacularly bad timing for Rockstar or a preview nobody asked for. Either way, expect the internet to have opinions about the Leonida map before Rockstar ever meant to show it.

Reported as first covered by GamesRadar and GameSpot.
