No, GTA6 Has Not Been Delayed Again — A Fake Post Is Behind The Rumor

📅 November 5, 2026 ✍️ Danny Marchetti ⏱️ 4 min read

Two weeks out from launch is exactly when fake delay rumors start spreading fastest — and right on schedule, one did. Here's the short version: no, GTA6 has not been delayed again.

🎯 What's Actually Going On

According to Insider Gaming, a social media post that appeared to come from Rockstar claimed GTA6 had been pushed back to March 2027. The post spread quickly online — but it was never an official Rockstar announcement. Rockstar's own official website still lists November 19, 2026 as the release date, unchanged. Insider Gaming's own reporting is blunt about it: unless something major has happened internally that hasn't been disclosed, GTA6 has not been delayed again.

🎮 Why This Matters

GTA6 has genuinely been delayed twice before — once in May 2025 and again in November 2025 — so a third delay isn't an outlandish idea on its face, which is exactly why a fake post claiming one spreads so easily. But "has been delayed before" isn't evidence of "has been delayed again." Always check Rockstar's own official channels before treating a viral screenshot as confirmed news.

🧭 Context: Why Delay Rumors Keep Showing Up

This isn't the first time speculation about a third delay has circulated — earlier reports have quoted industry figures saying another delay "can't be ruled out," and that kind of hedged, speculative commentary tends to get flattened into "GTA6 delayed" headlines by the time it spreads on social media. The difference this time is that the claim wasn't even speculation from an actual source — it was a fabricated post dressed up to look official.

📝 Editor's Note

Two weeks before launch is prime territory for this kind of thing — anxiety is high, everyone's refreshing for news, and a convincing-looking fake post travels faster than any correction can. The good news: Rockstar's own site is the single source of truth here, and it hasn't moved. November 19 stands.

The Bottom Line

GTA6 is still releasing November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. The March 2027 delay claim making the rounds traces back to a fake post, not a real Rockstar announcement — and Rockstar's own official channels confirm nothing has changed.
