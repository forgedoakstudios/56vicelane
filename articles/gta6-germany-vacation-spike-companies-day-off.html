Germany Sees 38% Spike In Vacation Requests For GTA6 Launch Week

📅 November 13, 2026 ✍️ Marcus Webb ⏱️ 5 min read

This isn't a fan survey or a vibes-based trend piece — it's actual HR booking data. Personio, the workforce-management platform used across Europe, says German vacation requests for GTA6 launch week are running 38% above average, with overtime-reduction requests up 58%. Pulled from anonymized bookings across 1.6 million employees, that's about as close to a hard number as this kind of story gets.

📊 The Actual Numbers

According to Personio's own data — reported across German outlets including ComputerBase, PCGames.de, and t3n — the spike centers on November 19 itself and runs into the following Thursday and Friday, suggesting plenty of workers are planning a real multi-day stretch with the game rather than just clocking out early on launch night. Germany isn't the single highest-spiking country in Personio's dataset (other markets show even bigger jumps), but a confirmed 38% is a genuinely large shift in booking behavior for one release date.

🏢 Companies Are Planning Around It Too

This isn't just workers quietly booking PTO — some employers are building the date into how they operate. Burger Motorsports, a California automotive tuning shop, publicly announced it's temporarily suspending operations on November 19 after multiple staff flagged scheduling conflicts around the launch, with a memo joking that employees would be "unavailable, unreachable, or in Vice City." It's the most visible case tracked across multiple outlets so far, and estimates circulating from HR-adjacent coverage put total 2026 projected U.S. time-off requests for the date in the seven figures.

🎮 What This Means

Take all of this as a real signal, not a punchline: when a workforce-platform's own anonymized booking data shows a measurable spike tied to a single release date, that's a genuinely unusual data point for the games industry. It also lines up with everything else pointing at GTA6's launch window being treated less like "a big game came out" and more like an actual cultural event people are structuring their calendars around.

📝 Editor's Note

Worth being precise about what this does and doesn't prove: a spike in booked vacation time around a date doesn't prove every one of those days is specifically "for GTA6" — people take time off around holidays and long weekends for plenty of reasons. But a measurable, dated, percentage-specific jump tied directly to launch week, reported by the HR platform itself rather than estimated by a fan outlet, is about as solid as this genre of story gets.

The Bottom Line

Six days out, and the clearest signal yet that GTA6's launch is being treated as a real event rather than just another release date: actual booking data showing a 38% jump in German vacation requests, plus employers publicly building the date into their own operations. Expect more of this as November 19 gets closer.

Reported as covered by ComputerBase and PC Games, citing Personio's own HR booking data.
