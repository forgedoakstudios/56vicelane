GTA6 Presales Reportedly Cross $1 Billion — One Week Before Launch

📅 November 11, 2026 ✍️ Marcus Webb ⏱️ 5 min read
📝 Update — November 11, 2026

Since this went up, it's become clear the headline number here undersells it. Take-Two's own guidance to investors — not a third-party estimate — puts the launch-period figure at up to $3.5 billion, a figure the company gave on the record during its Q2 FY2027 earnings call. Read that full story here — it's the more authoritative number, straight from the company, and it supersedes the Sensor Tower estimate below. We're leaving the original reporting intact below for the record, but treat Take-Two's own $3.5B guidance as the number that matters.

One week out from launch, and GTA6 has reportedly already crossed a billion dollars in presales — before a single copy has shipped. Here's what's actually being claimed, who's making the claim, and why that distinction matters.

💬 What's Actually Being Reported

According to Insider Gaming, mobile and games analytics firm Sensor Tower estimates GTA6 has already racked up more than 10.31 million units reserved worldwide, worth over $1 billion in presale revenue — a full week before the November 19 launch. That's a real milestone if it holds up, but it's worth being precise about where the number actually comes from.

This isn't an official figure from Rockstar or Take-Two. Neither company has disclosed exact presale numbers at any point in this entire cycle — that's been deliberate, and we've covered why before. What we do have on record is Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick telling Variety ahead of the company's Q2 FY2027 earnings call that sales are "far in excess of our expectations" — real confirmation that something big is happening, just not a number attached to it.

🧮 Sensor Tower vs. The Official Silence

Sensor Tower's $1 billion / 10.31 million-unit estimate is the latest in a string of third-party projections that have been climbing for months. We reported an earlier Sensor Tower estimate putting the figure above $400 million weeks ago, and separately, some analysts have projected launch week alone could bring in up to $5 billion once day-one sales are added to presales. If this new number is accurate, it's a straight-line continuation of that trend, not an outlier.

That said, estimate-stacking has burned this story before. We've also covered Newzoo publicly calling an earlier billion-dollar presale claim "absurd" back when it surfaced months ahead of any real sales window. The difference this time: we're one week from launch, not twenty, and the broader trendline of climbing estimates plus Zelnick's own "far in excess" comment make a billion-dollar number at this specific point in the cycle considerably more plausible than it was back then.

🎮 What This Means For You

Nothing changes for your own pre-order either way — pricing, availability, and the November 19 date are unaffected by any of this. What it does signal: demand is tracking at or above the most optimistic projections anyone's made so far, which makes server load and launch-day congestion worth planning around. If you haven't locked down your pre-load plan yet, our pre-load guide covers exactly when you can start downloading.

📝 Editor's Note

Marcus is right to flag the sourcing here, and it's worth repeating plainly: "Sensor Tower estimates" and "Take-Two confirms" are two very different sentences, and only one of them has actually happened. That doesn't mean the number is wrong — Sensor Tower has a real track record modeling this stuff, and Zelnick's own "far in excess of expectations" line is corroborating, if vague. It just means treat the headline number as a strong, well-sourced estimate, not an earnings-call fact, until Take-Two actually says it themselves.

The Bottom Line

If Sensor Tower's number holds, GTA6 has crossed $1 billion in presales before a single player has touched the finished game — a genuinely historic figure for the industry. But "if it holds" is doing real work in that sentence: this is a third-party estimate, not a Take-Two disclosure, and this exact kind of claim has been disputed by analysts before. The safest read: directionally, the hype and the money both appear to be real and climbing fast. The exact number is still an estimate until Take-Two says otherwise.
