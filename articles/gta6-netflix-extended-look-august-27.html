GTA 6 Is Coming To Netflix — "An Extended Look" Premieres August 27

August 6, 2026  ·  56ViceLane News Desk  ·  5 min read

Rockstar Games and Netflix just announced Grand Theft Auto VI: An Extended Look — a first-of-its-kind partnership premiering the next major look at GTA 6 exclusively on Netflix before it goes free everywhere else. Mark August 27. Here's everything confirmed, down to the minute.

— Confirmed Premiere Schedule, Thursday August 27 —
3:00 PM ET
Netflix Exclusive
9:00 PM ET
Free On YouTube + GTA6.com
Source: Rockstar Games and Netflix official announcement, August 6, 2026. Also 2 PM CT / 12 PM PT (Netflix) and 8 PM CT / 6 PM PT (YouTube).

What Was Actually Announced

Rockstar Games confirmed on X: "Grand Theft Auto VI: An Extended Look premieres on @netflix Thursday, August 27 at 3 p.m. ET." Netflix followed with its own confirmation, describing it as "a first-of-its-kind partnership to premiere an extended look at the next evolution in the groundbreaking Grand Theft Auto series."

Six hours after the Netflix premiere, the exact same presentation goes free for everyone — no subscription required — on Rockstar's official YouTube channel and the Grand Theft Auto VI website, at 9 p.m. ET / 6 p.m. PT. If you don't have Netflix, you are not locked out. You just wait until that evening.

Why This Matters

This is the first time a major game publisher has premiered a flagship trailer or gameplay reveal on a streaming service before releasing it on its own channels. Netflix is treating this like a piece of its own original content slate — not a paid promotion slot. That is a genuinely new marketing playbook, and Rockstar picking GTA 6 to launch it tells you how much weight this release is expected to carry.

Is This Trailer 3?

Officially, Rockstar and Netflix are calling it "An Extended Look," not a trailer. That distinction matters. Several outlets covering the announcement — including GameSpot and Vice — are already describing it in their headlines as a gameplay trailer, and the "extended" framing in the title strongly suggests something longer and deeper than the roughly 90-second cinematic drops of Trailers 1 and 2. Rockstar has not confirmed a runtime.

Whether Rockstar ends up calling this "Trailer 3" later or treats it as its own distinct reveal, the practical effect for fans is the same: this is the next confirmed, official look at GTA 6, and it has a locked date for the first time. Everything the community has been tracking — release windows, teaser hints, marketing-timeline math — was building toward exactly this kind of announcement.

What We Were Tracking

56ViceLane had been following Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick's "summertime" marketing comments and projecting a late-June to mid-July window for the next major reveal (see our Trailer 3 window breakdown and timing analysis). Rockstar ran later than that window — August 27 is well past mid-July — which is a useful reminder that even CEO-level signals only get you a range, not a date. Now we have the actual date, straight from Rockstar and Netflix, no reading between the lines required.

How To Watch — Every Option

  • Have Netflix: Watch it first, 3 p.m. ET / 2 p.m. CT / 12 p.m. PT, Thursday August 27, directly in the Netflix app.
  • Don't have Netflix: Watch it free, 9 p.m. ET / 8 p.m. CT / 6 p.m. PT, same day, on Rockstar's YouTube channel or the official GTA6 website.
  • Either way: It's the same presentation. Netflix just gets first access for six hours.
