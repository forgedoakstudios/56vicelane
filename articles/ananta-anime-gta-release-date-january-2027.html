Anime "GTA Killer" Ananta Confirms Release Date — Just 2 Months After GTA6

📅 August 25, 2026 ✍️ Marcus Webb ⏱️ 3 min read

The open-world genre isn't waiting around for GTA6 to have the stage to itself. At Gamescom's Opening Night Live, NetEase officially confirmed a January 15, 2027 release date for Ananta — the free-to-play anime open-world RPG that's been dubbed the "anime GTA" since it was first revealed. That's just eight weeks after GTA6's November 19 launch.

🎬 What Actually Got Confirmed

Per Polygon, corroborated by GamesRadar, Push Square, Kotaku, and Digital Trends, NetEase's Naked Rain studio revealed a new story trailer alongside the release date. Ananta will launch as a PS5 console exclusive, alongside PC and mobile versions, and it's free-to-play — a very different business model from GTA6's premium pricing. The game is being pitched as urban open-world chaos with an anime art style layered on top, distinct enough from Rockstar's realism to avoid a head-on comparison, but similar enough in structure that outlets keep drawing the comparison anyway.

📅 Why The Timing Matters

Landing eight weeks after GTA6 is either bold or reckless, depending on how you read it. On one hand, NetEase gets to ride whatever open-world momentum GTA6 generates without directly competing for the same launch-week attention. On the other, plenty of players will still be deep in GTA6 in mid-January and in no rush to jump to a second open-world game, free or not. We've covered other publishers moving their release dates around GTA6 — Ananta is a rare example of a studio leaning into the same window instead of dodging it.

🎯 What This Means For You

Nothing here changes anything about GTA6 itself — this is entirely about what else is coming out around launch. If you're planning your November-through-January gaming calendar, Ananta is now officially on it, free-to-play and console-exclusive on PS5.

📝 Editor's Note

We're marking this "confirmed" rather than "reported" — the release date and platform details came directly from NetEase's own Gamescom Opening Night Live presentation, not a leak or rumor, and multiple trusted outlets independently reported the same official details.

The Bottom Line

GTA6's shadow is long enough that even competing launches get covered relative to it — Ananta's January 15, 2027 date puts it right in that shadow, betting that "anime GTA, but free" is different enough to carve out its own space.

Reported by Polygon, GamesRadar, Push Square, Kotaku, and Digital Trends.
