Trevor's Take: 10 Days And I've Started Writing Things Down

📅 November 9, 2026 ⏱️ 6 min read
Trevor
Unsolicited, As Always

Ten days. I'm writing things down now. I bought a little notebook — not a bit, I actually drove to a store and purchased an actual notebook with actual money, the kind with a elastic strap, like I'm twelve and starting a new school — and I've started keeping a list. Danny asked what the list was for and I said "in case" and he said "in case of what" and I said "in case" again, louder, and he went back to his desk and I heard a drawer open. We're not doing great over here. I want to be upfront about that before you read the rest of this.

Yesterday I told you I'd started checking who's behind me. Update: I've now started checking who's behind the people who are behind me. This is not sustainable, I am aware, I don't need the emails. But I also can't stop, because the closer this gets, the more it feels like the whole bit — the column, the voice, the "unsolicited, as always" — was always a cover for something, and covers don't get quiet endings. Covers get blown. I don't know what mine blowing looks like. I just know I'll probably be the last to find out, which feels on brand.

📓 What's Actually In The Notebook

Fine. Since you asked, or didn't, doesn't matter, I'm telling you anyway: a list of people to thank, a list of people to NOT thank, The Kid's actual real name in case I need to explain to someone in an official capacity who he is, and a drawing of a windbreaker with a question mark over it that I am not going to elaborate on further. That's the whole notebook. Four pages. I have ten days to fill the rest and frankly I'm scared of what's going in there by day eight.

Here's the part I need on the record, properly, today, because I don't trust tomorrow-me to say it right: apparently there's a full GTA6 takeover happening at a department store in London right now. Selfridges. Merch wall, the whole production. That's not a leak anymore, that's not a rumor anymore, that's a building, in a real city, with real GTA6 stuff in it that real people can walk up and touch. That did something to me. I've been treating this whole thing like a story I was covering from a safe distance and apparently the story has a physical location now and it is actively being stocked with merchandise. The operation isn't winding down. The operation is setting up a retail display. I don't know what to do with that information except put it in the notebook.

Also there's supposedly 55 trophies now, including a Platinum, and some files got dug up showing big changes to GTA Online — unconfirmed, allegedly, according to people who are definitely more confirmed about things than I am right now — and I want to be a professional about this and say "interesting development" but what I actually feel is closer to "ten days, ten days, ten days" on a loop, which isn't a take, I know, I'm working on it.

🫡 A Message, Just In Case (Updated)

Everything from yesterday's message still stands — The Kid still owes me, sign up for The Last Drive, all of it. New addition: if the notebook turns up anywhere without me, don't read page four out loud at my memorial, assuming there is one, assuming any of this requires one, which it doesn't, I'm FINE. Last Drive's still free, still November 18th, still the one thing about all of this that actually calms me down. Come drive with me. I mean that more than usual.

Anyway. Ten days. I'm going to go add "buy a bigger notebook" to the notebook, which I recognize is a closed loop with no exit, and then I'm going to come back tomorrow and do this again, because that's the job now, apparently, until the 19th, notebook and all.

— Trevor. (Still here. Writing it down now, just in case.)
