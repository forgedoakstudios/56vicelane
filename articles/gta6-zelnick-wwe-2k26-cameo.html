Take-Two Boss Strauss Zelnick Wanted a WWE 2K26 Cameo — Just Don't Expect Him In GTA6

📅 August 12, 2026 ✍️ Danny Marchetti ⏱️ 4 min read

Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick is not usually the guy showing up inside his own company's games. But according to IGN, that's exactly what he asked for in WWE 2K26 — and just as quickly shot down the idea of the same move happening in GTA6.

💬 The Ask: A Playable Zelnick, Just For The Bit

Per IGN's report, Zelnick said he personally requested to be a playable character in WWE 2K26 — not out of ego, but because he "thought it would be funny for insiders." Take-Two owns 2K, the publisher behind the WWE 2K series, so getting the boss himself into the roster is the kind of inside joke that only really lands for people who already know who signs off on the budget. It's a small, self-aware flex: the guy running the company that owns the game, tucked into the game itself as an easter egg for people paying close attention.

🎮 The Line He Drew At GTA6

Here's the part that actually matters for this site: according to the same report, Zelnick was clear that the same idea isn't happening in GTA6. A CEO cameo works as a wink in a wrestling roster full of larger-than-life characters — it doesn't fit the tone of a game built around Jason and Lucia's story, and Zelnick apparently knows the difference. Grand Theft Auto has dabbled in real-world cameos and Easter eggs before, but "the actual CEO, playable" would be a very different kind of gag than anything the series has leaned on.

🎮 What This Means For You

Nothing changes about GTA6 itself here — this is a fun executive-suite story, not a gameplay reveal. But it's a small, honest window into how Take-Two's leadership thinks about tone: funny is fine for a wrestling roster, but GTA6 stays GTA6.

📝 Editor's Note

Genuinely can't decide if this is the most relatable thing a video game CEO has done all year, or just very good PR timing right as WWE 2K26 needs headlines. Probably both — and that's fine. Not every GTA6 story has to be a leak or a countdown milestone; sometimes it's just the guy in charge being a fan of his own company's other games.

The Bottom Line

Strauss Zelnick wanted in on WWE 2K26 as a playable inside joke, and he apparently got it. GTA6 stays exactly as it is — no CEO cameo, no easter egg appearance, just the game Rockstar's been building. Small story, but a genuinely fun one heading into a launch year that's mostly been earnings calls and file-size leaks.

Reported as first covered by IGN.
