GTA6's Trophy List: 55 Achievements? Not So Fast

📅 November 9, 2026 ✍️ Danny Marchetti ⏱️ 4 min read

A number's been making the rounds today: 55. As in, 55 trophies and achievements supposedly waiting inside GTA6 — reportedly more than GTA5 or Red Dead Redemption 2 shipped with at launch. We went looking for where that number actually comes from, and it's worth slowing down before you treat it as settled.

🏆 Where The "55" Number Is Coming From

According to Eurogamer, GTA6 is set to ship with 55 trophies and achievements — reportedly surpassing both GTA5 and Red Dead Redemption 2's launch-day totals. For context, Red Dead Redemption 2 launched with 51 Xbox achievements and 52 PlayStation trophies (including the platinum), so a 55-item list would be a modest but real step up for Rockstar's completionist crowd.

That's the claim as reported. Here's the problem: Rockstar hasn't published an official trophy or achievement list for GTA6, and when we went looking for a second source to back up that specific figure, we couldn't find one. Worse, at least one other outlet covering the same general topic has floated a number well over 100 trophies — which flatly contradicts the 55 figure, and reads more like speculation than anything Rockstar has confirmed.

🧮 What We Can Actually Confirm

Nothing official, yet. No trophy or achievement list exists from Rockstar or PlayStation/Xbox platform holders as of this writing. Any specific count you see right now — 55, 100-plus, or otherwise — should be read as a guess or early report, not a locked-in fact. A PlayStation Platinum trophy is a safe bet (it's awarded automatically for collecting every other trophy in a game), but the exact number underneath it isn't nailed down.

🤔 Why We're Not Just Running With It

We'll be honest about our process here: a single-source claim with a specific number, no second outlet confirming the same figure, and at least one conflicting figure floating around elsewhere is exactly the combination that should make you pump the brakes before treating something as fact — even when the headline is fun and harmless either way. Trophy counts aren't exactly a smoking-gun leak, but getting the habit right matters the same regardless of stakes.

📝 Editor's Note

We're running this because the underlying question — "how many trophies will GTA6 have?" — is a genuinely fun thing for completionists to speculate about this close to launch, and because being transparent about what's actually confirmed versus what's floating around is more useful to you than pretending a number is locked in when it isn't. We'll update this piece the moment Rockstar or the platform holders publish something official.

The Bottom Line

Treat "55 trophies" as an unconfirmed report, not a fact, until Rockstar or Sony/Microsoft publish an actual list. We'll be watching for that alongside everything else as launch week approaches.
