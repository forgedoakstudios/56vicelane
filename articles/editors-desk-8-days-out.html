From The Editor's Desk: 8 Days Out

📅 November 11, 2026 ⏱️ 7 min read
Danny Marchetti
Editor-in-Chief

Eight days. I said nine felt like the last day I could squint and call it "basically two digits." Eight does not permit squinting. Eight is just eight, small, insistent, sitting there on the calendar refusing to be rounded into anything more comfortable. I noticed the window outside my office has gone that particular shade of late-afternoon orange that I've started associating with deadline week specifically, which is either a sign I've been in this building too many consecutive evenings, or a sign the building itself has started trying to tell me something. I'm not ruling out either.

I also want to flag, for the record, that yesterday I set a personal limit of three antacid drawers and called it final. I have opened a fourth drawer. I want to be very clear that the three-drawer rule has not been broken — I have found what I am choosing to call a loophole, which is that the rule applied to my tolerance for questions about Trevor's notebook, not to actual physical drawers of stomach medicine, and I would like whoever is keeping score at home to note that distinction, because I worked on it for a while and I'm proud of it.

🌐 We Apparently Strained The Internet A Little

According to Dexerto, GTA6's preload is already causing real, measurable internet slowdowns as players rush to get the file onto their drives early — which, first of all, incredible, we did that, a video game that isn't even out yet is putting a dent in global bandwidth, and second of all, I would like whoever is reading this at their ISP to know we are sorry, it's not us specifically, it's all of us collectively, there's a difference, I think.

💵 "A Bargain," Reportedly

Also making the rounds: Take-Two's leadership, pointing at the $80 price tag amid higher gas and grocery prices, calling GTA6 a "bargain" by comparison, according to GameSpot's coverage. I want to be fair to the argument — entertainment-hours-per-dollar, sure, I understand the math, I'm not actually disputing the math. I just think there's a very specific kind of corporate confidence required to describe your own $80 product as the responsible choice in a tight economy, and I respect it, the way you respect a magic trick you can't quite follow even after someone explains it to you twice.

🔍 Editor's Note — A Correction, Sort Of

We also saw a headline this week claiming GTA6 is "officially" the most pre-ordered game in PlayStation and Xbox store history, Sony and Microsoft confirming. We looked into it properly before running with it, because that's the job: near as we and several other outlets can tell, nobody — not Sony, not Microsoft, not Take-Two — has actually released real pre-order figures. The "record" framing appears to be a PR line, not a confirmed number. We're not running an article on it. I'd rather tell you honestly that something doesn't hold up than hand you a headline that feels good for a day and falls apart by the weekend.

What We're Doing About It

Same as every day this week: daily Editor's Desk, daily Trevor's Take, straight through to the 19th, no exceptions, no days off, because apparently that was the deal I made with myself back when eight days still sounded like a lot of days. I've also started reading headlines standing up instead of sitting down, on the theory that it's easier to immediately pace off a bad one if I'm already vertical. Preliminary results: inconclusive, but my watch remains thrilled.

The bottom line

Eight days, a strained internet, an $80 "bargain," and a loophole I'm quietly proud of. When a number like $80 gets called a bargain in the same week as a $3.5 billion forecast, does that framing land for you, or does it just sound like a company talking to investors in one sentence and players in the next?
