GTA6 Believed To Have Sold Almost 4.5 Million Pre-Orders

📅 August 19, 2026 ✍️ Marcus Webb ⏱️ 3 min read

Take-Two still won't put a real number on GTA6 pre-orders, but that hasn't stopped analysts from doing the math themselves. The latest estimate: nearly 4.5 million copies pre-ordered, months before the game is even out.

🔢 Where The Number Comes From

According to The Gamer, industry analysts believe GTA6 has sold close to 4.5 million pre-order copies so far. This is an estimate, not an official figure — Take-Two has explicitly declined to publish raw pre-order numbers, so outside analysts are working from retailer signals, regional sell-through data, and comparable launch patterns rather than a confirmed count straight from the source. We're treating this as a credible estimate, not a hard fact, until Take-Two says otherwise.

💰 Why That Number Matters Even As An Estimate

Even on the low end of "believed to be," 4.5 million pre-orders this far out from launch would already put GTA6 in genuinely historic territory — we've previously covered analyst projections putting total launch-week revenue as high as $5 billion. An estimate like this one is a data point supporting that broader thesis, not a standalone bombshell: it's more confirmation that the demand signals analysts have been tracking all year are holding up.

🎯 What This Means For You

If you've already pre-ordered, nothing changes for you — this is a market-watching story, not a product update. If you're still deciding, take it as one more data point suggesting supply could tighten as November 19 gets closer, not a reason to panic-buy today.

⚠️ A Necessary Grain Of Salt

We've been here before this year — one analyst firm's earlier pre-order claims got called "absurd" by a rival research shop, and the truth ended up somewhere in between "hype" and "dismissal." Estimates in a pre-order market this opaque are genuinely useful directional signals, but they're not receipts. Until Take-Two, Rockstar, or a major retailer confirms a real figure, every number floating around — including this one — should be read as "plausible," not "settled."

📝 Editor's Note

We keep covering these dueling estimates because the pattern itself is the story: a company staying deliberately quiet on numbers while outside analysts race to fill the silence with their own math. Whatever the real figure turns out to be, expect more of these stories between now and November 19.

The Bottom Line

Nearly 4.5 million pre-orders, if the estimate holds, is a big number by any measure — but it's still an outside estimate filling a gap Take-Two has chosen not to fill itself. Worth watching, not worth treating as gospel just yet.

Reported as first covered by The Gamer.
