There's No Official GTA6 Headset Yet — But The LucidSound LS500 Gets Close

📅 August 18, 2026 ✍️ Julian Ashworth ⏱️ 3 min read

Rockstar and Take-Two haven't licensed a single piece of official GTA6 hardware yet — no headset, no controller, nothing with the game's actual branding on it. But LucidSound just launched a colorway that gets closer to "official GTA6 gear" than anything with a real license so far, and it's called, appropriately, "Sunset Paradise."

🎨 What's Actually New Here

According to GamesRadar, LucidSound's LS500 wireless headset now ships in a new "Sunset Paradise" finish — a pink-to-orange gradient with palm-adjacent styling that reads unmistakably like Vice City at golden hour. To be clear: this is LucidSound's own design language, not a Rockstar or Take-Two collaboration, and there's no GTA6 logo, no in-game tie-in, no official licensing involved. It's a coincidence of good timing and good taste, not a partnership.

🌅 Why It Still Feels Like The Real Thing

That's exactly what makes it interesting. Nine years — okay, thirteen years — of GTA6 anticipation has apparently trained peripheral makers to reach for a very specific palette without anyone asking them to: hot pink, burnt orange, that late-'80s sunset gradient the whole franchise is built on. LucidSound didn't need a licensing deal to land on "this is what GTA6 fans want to look at," because at this point the aesthetic has basically become public domain in gamers' heads.

🎯 What This Means For You

If you're gear-shopping ahead of November 19 and want something that looks the part without waiting on an official (and likely more expensive) licensed product down the line, this is a real option worth a look — not a marketing stunt, an actual shipping product. Just don't expect in-game perks or any Rockstar tie-in, because there isn't one.

🕹️ The Bigger Pattern

We've already tracked how far GTA6 anticipation reaches into places you wouldn't expect — from our own buyer's guide breaking down the current best-in-class headsets for the game specifically, to the tech obsessives dissecting exactly how GTA6 will run down to the frame. Peripheral makers riding the aesthetic without a license is just the next stage of the same phenomenon — the game hasn't shipped yet, and it's already shaping what gear looks like on store shelves.

📝 Editor's Note

Worth remembering: colorway hype like this moves fast and companies know it. Don't mistake "looks like GTA6" for "endorsed by GTA6" — always check the actual product listing for licensing language before assuming a tie-in that isn't there.

The Bottom Line

No official GTA6 hardware exists yet, and there's no telling if or when that changes. But if you want something on your desk right now that captures the mood without waiting on Rockstar's blessing, LucidSound's "Sunset Paradise" LS500 is the closest unofficial answer so far — proof that the aesthetic itself has become bigger than any one license.

Reported as first covered by GamesRadar.
