GTA6 Isn't On PC Yet. A Gaming Laptop Still Makes Sense

Worth saying plainly: GTA6 launches November 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only. PC isn't confirmed until 2027. So this isn't a "be ready for launch day" guide — it's a "here's what to have ready for whenever Rockstar actually ships the PC version" guide, and why a gaming laptop earns its keep in the meantime regardless.

By 56ViceLane Staff  ·  July 28, 2026

Our PC build guide covers the desktop route for whenever that 2027 PC release actually happens. When the PC version does land, you're not starting from zero.

Laptop vs. Building

Real talk on the tradeoff: a desktop at the same performance tier is almost always the better dollar-for-dollar deal — you're not paying for portability. But a laptop wins if you actually value being able to move it, if you don't have desk space, or if the idea of picking parts and assembling them sounds like a chore instead of a hobby. Neither answer is wrong. Pick based on how you'll actually use it, not which one sounds more hardcore.
