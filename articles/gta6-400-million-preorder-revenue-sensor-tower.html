GTA6 Has Already Made $400 Million-Plus From Pre-Orders, Analysts Say

📅 August 19, 2026 ✍️ Marcus Webb ⏱️ 4 min read

Take-Two still hasn't given anyone a hard, official number — but a fresh third-party estimate suggests GTA6 pre-orders have already crossed the $400 million mark. And this time, the estimate has company: multiple independent outlets landed on nearly identical figures within hours of each other.

💵 The Numbers Being Reported

According to GameSpot, third-party mobile/sales analytics firm Sensor Tower estimates GTA6 has moved roughly 4.38 million pre-order copies, generating approximately $429 million in revenue. The breakdown reportedly skews heavily toward PlayStation 5 — around 3.38 million copies (~$331 million) versus roughly 1 million on Xbox Series X|S (~$98 million), a 77/23 split. The pricier $100 Ultimate Edition is said to be dominating the mix over the standard $80 version.

🎙️ What Take-Two Itself Has Actually Said

This isn't coming out of nowhere. On Take-Two's August 7 earnings call, CEO Strauss Zelnick himself called pre-order demand "astronomical" and "unprecedented" — an on-record characterization from the company, even without a specific dollar figure attached. That's the closest thing to an official confirmation this story has: Take-Two's own leadership backing the general shape of the claim, while the exact number comes entirely from outside analytics rather than the company's books.

🎯 What This Means For You

Nothing changes for anyone who's already pre-ordered. If you're still weighing it, this is one more signal that demand is real and significant, even if the precise dollar total is still analyst math rather than a Take-Two press release.

⚠️ Sensor Tower Isn't Rockstar's Books

Worth being direct about the limits here: Sensor Tower's estimates are built from its own tracking methodology, not access to Rockstar or Take-Two's actual sales ledgers. We've covered before how dueling analyst firms can land on wildly different numbers for the same underlying reality, so treat "$429 million" as a credible, well-corroborated estimate — multiple outlets converged on nearly the same figure independently — rather than a confirmed fact.

📝 Editor's Note

What makes this one feel sturdier than some earlier pre-order chatter is the convergence: several separate outlets landed on essentially the same number from the same underlying Sensor Tower data, and it lines up with Zelnick's own on-record language. That's a meaningfully stronger signal than a single outlet's lone estimate — but "stronger signal" still isn't "confirmed number."

The Bottom Line

Somewhere in the neighborhood of $400-430 million in pre-order revenue, months before launch, is a genuinely enormous number by any measure — and it's backed by both third-party analytics and Take-Two's own "unprecedented" language. Just don't mistake analyst math for an audited quarterly report; that's still Take-Two's to publish, whenever it chooses to.

Reported as first covered by GameSpot.
