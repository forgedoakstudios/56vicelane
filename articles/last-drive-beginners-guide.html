How The Last Drive Actually Works: A Beginner's Guide

Never joined a community drive before? Here's exactly how it works — no confusion, no guessing — from picking a platform to actually showing up on November 18.

56ViceLane Staff  •  Guide · Community

The Last Drive is simple in concept — one final cruise through GTA5, together, the night before GTA6 launches. No killing, no chaos, just the road. But if you've never signed up for a community event like this before, the actual signup page can look like more than it is. Here's the whole thing broken down, step by step.

Step 1: Pick Your Platform

The Last Drive runs across every major platform GTA5 is still live on: PS5, PS4, Xbox Series X/S, Xbox One, and PC. You'll only ever see and join drives happening on your own platform — there's no cross-platform play involved, so pick whichever one you actually play on and everything else follows from there.

Step 2: Pick Your Region

Drives are organized by region so the timing actually makes sense for wherever you live. The six regions are:

All times you see after picking a region are already shown in that region's local timezone — you don't have to do any conversion math yourself.

Step 3: Pick A Time Slot

Each region runs four time slots the night of the event, so there's room for early risers and night owls alike:

6:00 PM
Evening Cruise
8:00 PM
Night Cruise
10:00 PM
Late Night
12:00 AM
Midnight Send-Off

You're not locked into one specific slot forever — if your plans change, you can always pick a different one before the night actually arrives.

Step 4: Lead Or Join

1Join a Drive

The easiest path for a first-timer. Pick your platform, region, and time slot, and you'll be dropped into an existing drive that's already forming, with a staging area and chat set up for you automatically.

2Lead a Drive

If you want to organize your own crew for a specific slot — maybe you're bringing a Discord group or a clan — leading lets you set up a fresh drive that others can then discover and join. You don't need any special status to lead one; anyone can.

What Actually Happens That Night

Once you've picked a drive, you'll get a staging time and a chat to coordinate in beforehand — meeting point, formation, whatever your group wants to work out. Then at your slot's start time, it's exactly what it sounds like: a cruise through the streets of GTA5, together, one last time. No objectives, no killing, no chaos — the whole point is the drive itself, not a mission layered on top of it.

Bottom Line

Platform, region, time slot, lead or join — that's the entire decision tree. Takes about a minute to sign up, and you can always change your slot later if your plans shift before November 18.
