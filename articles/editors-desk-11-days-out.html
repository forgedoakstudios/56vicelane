From The Editor's Desk: 11 Days Out

📅 November 8, 2026 ⏱️ 6 min read
Danny Marchetti
Editor-in-Chief

Good morning. Eleven days. I wrote that number down on a sticky note this morning and just looked at it for a while before I could make myself start typing anything else. Eleven days until GTA6 launches, which means eleven days until four months of running this desk either paid off or didn't, and I'll be honest with you — I have started keeping antacids in the drawer where the stapler used to live. That's not a bit. That's an inventory change I made to this desk, physically, with my hands, because the stapler lost the argument.

So starting today, you're getting us every single day until launch. Not just me — Trevor too, which I recognize is either the best decision I've made all year or the one that finally breaks something in this building, and at this point I genuinely don't know which and I've stopped trying to guess. One Editor's Desk, one Trevor's Take, daily, all the way to November 19th. If the news is slow some days, we'll talk about the site instead. If the news isn't slow, God help all of us.

📈 Where We Actually Stand

Quick gut-check on numbers, because I think it's easy to lose sight of this when you're this deep in the grind: 166 registered drivers for The Last Drive, over 100 people in the Discord, and a site that didn't exist four months ago. I keep having to remind myself of that when I'm three energy drinks in at 11 PM arguing with myself about whether a headline is overselling a rumor. We built something. It's small, and it's real, and in eleven days it either gets a lot bigger or it was still worth every late night getting here. I've made my peace with either outcome. Mostly.

🗞️ What Actually Happened This Week

Take-Two's boss is out there publicly comparing our wait to Cyberpunk 2077's, which — I read that one twice, because that's either a reassuring comparison or a deeply ominous one depending on your mood, and my mood this week has not been generous. We've also got another batch of new screenshots making the rounds, which is the kind of thing that used to make my whole day in June and now just gets filed, fact-checked, and queued, because there isn't room left in my nervous system for "delighted" right now. There's also a graffiti artist apparently two-for-two now defacing GTA6 ad murals, which, delicately: if you're vandalizing an ad for a game you're clearly excited enough about to vandalize something over, maybe just... buy the game.

😬 Editor's Note

I want to be straight with you about something: the closer we get, the more I notice myself re-reading our own headlines after they publish, looking for a word I'd change. That's new. That didn't used to happen. I used to hit publish and move on. Now I hit publish and sit there for a second like I just signed something. Four months in this chair and the job hasn't gotten easier, it's just gotten more... mine, I guess. Heavier because it's mine. Anyway. Rolaids. Top drawer. Moving on.

What's Changing Starting Today

Daily Editor's Desk, daily Trevor's Take, both running right up through launch day. Longer posts than we've been doing, because frankly there's more to say the closer this gets and I'd rather over-explain than leave you guessing what's actually going on behind the scenes here. If something breaks on the site, I'll tell you. If something's working, I'll tell you that too. If I need to take a walk around the block because my chest feels tight again, I'm telling Trevor and he is, apparently, going to tell all of you, so get ready for that.

The bottom line

Eleven days. We're not slowing down and neither is the news. What do you want covered between now and launch — more Last Drive logistics, more news breakdowns, or more of whatever's happening with Trevor, who I can hear pacing in the next room as I type this?
