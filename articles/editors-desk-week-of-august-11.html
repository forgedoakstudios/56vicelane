From The Editor's Desk: Week Of August 11

📅 August 11, 2026 ⏱️ 4 min read
Danny Marchetti
Editor-in-Chief

Good morning. Short one this week, because the news is mostly about us for once: we just brought on several new staff writers, and I want you to actually know who's writing what you read here, instead of everything just showing up under one generic byline.

👋 Meet The New Desk

Each of them owns a real beat — you'll start seeing their names on the stories that actually fit their lane, not scattered randomly:

Marcus Webb
Business, pre-orders, sales records, Take-Two earnings
Julian Ashworth
Hardware, file size, performance, PC/console specs
Ezra Voss
Leaks, lore, map mysteries, the conspiracy-board stuff
Camille Duarte
Character design, fashion, cover-art breakdowns, culture
Reg Calloway
Retro/nostalgia, franchise history, "how far we've come"
✅ Why This Matters To You

Real names, real beats, means you can actually build trust with a specific voice instead of a faceless "News Desk" tag. If Julian's breaking down a leaked spec sheet, you know that's his actual lane. If Ezra's connecting dots on a corkboard, take it exactly as seriously as that sounds.

⚡ Faster On Breaking News

We also tightened up how fast real news turns into an actual article on this site instead of just sitting in a scan log nobody reads. Bigger, well-sourced stories get verified and go up the same day; everything else gets drafted and held for a final look before it ships. Point is: more real coverage, faster, without just blasting out anything that scores a keyword match.

The bottom line

New names, same standards. Go say hi in the comments — which staff writer's beat are you most excited to actually see bylined going forward?
