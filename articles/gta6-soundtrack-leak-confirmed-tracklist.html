GTA6's Leaked Soundtrack Just Got Confirmed Accurate, Days Before Launch

📅 November 15, 2026 ✍️ Ezra Voss ⏱️ 5 min read

A leaked GTA6 soundtrack list has been floating around since last night, and the usual instinct would be to wait for confirmation before treating it as real. Four days out, that confirmation already landed — and it landed fast.

🎧 The Leak, Then The Confirmation

The leak first surfaced via a tracklist report from IGN, naming a lineup that included Lana Del Rey, Playboi Carti, and Keith Richards. Within hours, PC Gamer reported the leak had been independently confirmed accurate — not "allegedly," not "sources suggest," confirmed. A follow-up report from Eurogamer expanded the picture further, describing a fuller 34-song list for what's being called "GTA6: The Album," adding names like Dua Lipa and Rina Sawayama to the mix.

That's an unusually fast turnaround for a leak-to-confirmation cycle this close to launch, and it's worth noting plainly: this is one of the cleaner leaks of the entire GTA6 cycle. No blurry phone footage, no anonymous forum post — just a tracklist that multiple outlets could independently verify within the same news cycle.

🎶 Why The Lineup Itself Is The Story

GTA's soundtracks have always doubled as a cultural snapshot of the game's setting, and a list spanning Lana Del Rey's moody Americana, Playboi Carti's chaos-rap, Keith Richards' rock pedigree, and Dua Lipa and Rina Sawayama's pop sensibilities suggests Vice City's in-game radio is aiming for the same genre-blending sprawl that made previous GTA soundtracks so quotable. It also tracks with how the series has used music before — Trailer 1's needle-drop wasn't an accident, as we covered when breaking down the Tom Petty deep cut behind that first reveal.

🎮 What This Means For You

If accurate, expect at least some of these tracks to be part of the in-game radio stations rather than a licensed one-off — Rockstar typically builds full fictional stations around real-world needle-drops rather than a single soundtrack album. Nothing to act on yet, but worth bookmarking if you're the type who wants the radio dial figured out before you even start the campaign.

📝 Editor's Note

What's genuinely notable here isn't the leak itself — leaks happen constantly this close to a launch this big — it's how quickly and cleanly it got corroborated. A tracklist this specific, confirmed this fast, by this many separate outlets, is about as close to settled fact as a pre-launch leak gets.

The Bottom Line

Four days before GTA6 ships, its soundtrack has effectively already leaked and been confirmed — a genuinely unusual, clean break from the typical "maybe fake, maybe real" leak cycle that's defined most of this launch window. We'll update this piece if Rockstar makes anything official before November 19.

As first reported by IGN, confirmed by PC Gamer, with further detail from Eurogamer.
