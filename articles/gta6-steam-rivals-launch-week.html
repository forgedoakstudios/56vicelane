Yes, Some Brave Games Are Still Launching On Steam During GTA6 Week

📅 November 14, 2026 ✍️ Marcus Webb ⏱️ 4 min read

Every release-window story we've covered this year has followed the same pattern: studios moving their own launches away from GTA6. According to PC Gamer's weekly Steam roundup, 32 games are launching on Steam during GTA6's actual launch week anyway — including one with a name that reads like it's daring Rockstar to notice.

🚗 Meet "Phantom Vice Auto"

PC Gamer's report singles out a game literally titled Phantom Vice Auto, launching on Steam the exact same day as GTA6 — November 19. PC Gamer frames it, fairly, as the closest thing PC players get to a GTA6-shaped experience on launch day itself, since Rockstar hasn't announced a PC version of GTA6 alongside the console release. Whether that's a cheeky coincidence or a very deliberate bit of positioning by its developer, the timing and the name both do a lot of the marketing on their own.

🎮 Reminder: GTA6 Still Has No Confirmed PC Date

Worth repeating plainly since it keeps getting buried under hype: Rockstar has not announced a PC release alongside November 19's console launch. GTA5's own PC version didn't arrive until well over a year after console — if GTA6 follows that pattern, PC players are looking at a long wait no matter what else launches this week.

🧮 32 Games, One Very Loud Week

PC Gamer's "brave enough to release on GTA6 day" framing is doing real work here — most of the 32 are smaller indie and niche titles that would ordinarily get overlooked in any week, GTA6-adjacent or not. Launching into the loudest week of the year is either a shrug ("nothing beats this anyway, might as well") or a bet that a sliver of displaced attention is still worth more than a quiet week with no competition at all.

The Bottom Line

32 Steam launches, one pointedly-named standout, and zero confirmed GTA6 PC release date to worry about colliding with. PC players who want something GTA-shaped on November 19 itself now technically have an option — it's just not actually GTA6.
