Some GTA6 Physical Copies Are Reportedly Shipping With Missing Or Already-Redeemed Codes

📅 November 15, 2026 ✍️ Julian Ashworth ⏱️ 5 min read

Four days before launch, the "disc is really just a download code" story we've been tracking has gotten a lot less theoretical. Buyers are now reporting that some sealed physical GTA6 copies are arriving with no code at all — or a code that's already been used by someone else.

📭 What Buyers Are Actually Finding

According to Polygon, pre-order customers who received physical Xbox and PS5 copies are opening sealed cases to find the code slot empty — one report described a PS5 buyer's inner sleeve showing a gray strip where the download code should have printed. Other buyers say their copy's code works fine on paper but gets rejected at checkout, because it had already been redeemed before the box ever reached them.

This is a real escalation from the story we covered a day earlier about codes being visible through sealed packaging under strong light — that was a security/privacy concern about a code being exposed. This is buyers reporting the code is missing or dead entirely, a different and more immediate problem for anyone trying to actually play the game they paid for.

Who's Taking Responsibility?

Reports describe a frustrating runaround: buyers who purchased from GameStop or Best Buy say both retailers pointed them back to Rockstar Games support, while Rockstar's own site tells third-party buyers to go back to the retailer. At least one account describes a Rockstar support ticket being closed without resolution, and a separate account describes Best Buy declining a refund. There's also a regional wrinkle — some reports say buyers outside the US haven't been able to redeem their codes at all, regardless of condition.

🎮 What This Means For You

If you've pre-ordered a physical copy, open the box and try the code the moment it arrives — don't wait until launch day to discover a problem, since support response times are already looking slow this close to release. Keep your receipt and the box itself; multiple reports point to retailers and Rockstar each needing proof before they'll act. And if you haven't ordered yet, this is a real argument in favor of going digital instead, at least for launch week.

📝 Editor's Note

How widespread this actually is depends on who you ask — some coverage frames it as a handful of unlucky boxes, others frame it as a bigger pattern. Either way, the timing is brutal: four days before launch is exactly when a support backlog like this is hardest to clear, and exactly when the most people are opening boxes for the first time.

The Bottom Line

We flagged weeks ago that a physical GTA6 disc "doesn't make sense" by Take-Two's own admission, and that a disc-as-download-code setup was the most likely outcome. That's exactly what shipped — and a code-only physical release means a missing or dead code isn't a minor annoyance, it's the entire product failing to work. Rockstar hasn't announced a comprehensive fix as of this writing; we'll update if that changes.

As reported by Polygon, with additional reports circulating across several outlets citing affected buyers.
