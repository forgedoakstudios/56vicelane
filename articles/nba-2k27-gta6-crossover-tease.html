NBA 2K27 Teases A GTA6 Crossover Season

📅 August 26, 2026 ✍️ Marcus Webb ⏱️ 3 min read

Take-Two owns both Rockstar and 2K, and its marketing team just decided to remind everyone of that fact. NBA 2K27's Season 3 trailer drops in visual cues that are unmistakably GTA6 — and the timing lines up almost too well to be a coincidence.

👀 What The Trailer Actually Shows

According to IGN, corroborated by GameSpot, Kotaku, and GTA Boom, the Season 3 trailer's camera drifts past a purple poster stacked with palm trees and police sirens, with "Season 3" rendered in a font and color scheme lifted straight from GTA6's own branding. An arcade cabinet stamped with the Rockstar Games logo sits right next to one wearing 2K's — a fairly unsubtle nod for a trailer that stops short of confirming anything outright.

📅 The Timing Isn't Subtle Either

NBA 2K27's Season 2 is locked for October 16, and 2K's recent seasons have run roughly six weeks each — which puts a Season 3 launch somewhere in late November. That's close enough to GTA6's November 19 release that it's hard to read as anything but deliberate: Take-Two using one of its biggest live-service properties to point players toward its biggest premium release right as it's landing.

🎯 What This Means For You

Nothing is officially confirmed yet — no content details, no exact date, no statement from Rockstar or 2K. If you play NBA 2K27, expect some kind of Vice-flavored content (courts, cosmetics, or a limited-time event) to show up right around GTA6's launch window. If you don't play it, this is mostly a signal of how hard Take-Two intends to cross-promote once GTA6 is actually out.

📝 Editor's Note

We're keeping this at "reported" — the trailer's visual references are real and independently confirmed across multiple outlets, but neither Rockstar nor 2K has issued an official statement describing what the crossover actually involves, so we're not treating any specifics as settled fact.

The Bottom Line

It's a tease, not an announcement — but Take-Two rarely puts a Rockstar-branded arcade cabinet in a trailer by accident. Expect more official word as GTA6's launch gets closer.
