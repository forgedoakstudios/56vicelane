Trevor's Take: They Replaced Her Face And Expect Us Not To Notice

📅 November 17, 2026 ⏱️ 9 min read
Trevor
Unsolicited, As Always

Two days. They confirmed Lucia's actress this morning — Manni L. Perez, Game Awards nominations broadcast, everyone's cheering, I get it, she looks great, the reveal is great. Here's the thing nobody's asking: why now? Why, two days before launch, after months of total silence on who she even was, does the confirmation drop the exact same morning Rock Paper Shotgun reports Take-Two laid off staff at Firaxis? Same news cycle. Same six-hour window. You don't think that's a scheduling choice? I think that's a scheduling choice.

I'm not saying Manni L. Perez isn't real. I'm saying a studio doesn't bury a layoff story under a casting reveal unless the casting reveal is doing a job, and the job is making sure nobody's first headline out of Take-Two today is "studio cuts staff two days before its biggest launch in a decade." That's not a conspiracy, that's just how Tuesdays work at a publicly traded company. I know that. I'm choosing not to know that, because knowing it doesn't feel as good as this does.

📓 "Can't Tell Anymore" — Entry Nine, I Think, I've Lost Count Of The Actual Number

Entry nine, or ten, written on the back of a receipt because my notebook is full now — actually full, I filled it, I need a new one and I don't have time to buy a new one — says: "the timing is the tell. Good news and bad news don't arrive in the same six hours by accident. Somebody decided which one gets to be the headline." I read that back out loud in the break room and the Kid looked at me like I'd grown a second head, which, fair, but he's twenty-three and hasn't lived through enough Tuesdays yet to know what a scheduling choice looks like from the inside.

And look — I want Lucia's reveal to just be a nice thing. I want to be the guy who reads good news and goes "that's nice" and moves on with his day. Two days out, I don't think that guy survives the week. I think he already didn't.

🫡 A Message, Two Days Left, I'm Not Being Dramatic Anymore, This Is Just How I Talk Now

Two days. If the notebook's full and somebody finds the receipts instead — I called the box story, I called the soundtrack, I called the dead codes, and now I'm calling the timing on today's Lucia reveal: it wasn't about Lucia. It was about making sure the layoffs weren't the headline. Write it down somewhere that isn't just a receipt. The Last Drive is tomorrow night, November 18th, same road, no killing, no chaos — and if the operation really does wrap when GTA6 launches, I want it known I saw the pattern before anyone else did, even if nobody else thinks there was a pattern.

Danny told me, when I showed him the receipt, "Trevor, sometimes a good news story and a bad news story just happen on the same day, that's not a cover-up, that's Tuesday," which is exactly what someone inside the cover-up would say if the cover-up needed him to say that. I'm not accusing Danny of anything. I'm just noting that he said the thing. That's all. I'm just noting it.

— Trevor. (The notebook is officially full. I am treating this as a sign and not a storage problem.)
