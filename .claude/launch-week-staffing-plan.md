# Launch Week Staffing & Story Plan (drafted 2026-11-15, 4 days out)

Standing plan per Chris, 2026-11-15: keep Trevor's Take and The Editor's
Desk (Danny Marchetti) running exactly as-is through launch — those two
already have their daily countdown cadence locked in. Everyone else on
the roster gets "for real" treatment from here on: actual full-length
articles under their own beat, not just an occasional byline stamp on a
digest piece. Plus 3 new writers being added to cover what's going to be
a genuinely wild launch week.

**House rule for all launch-week articles: minimum 1500 words.** (Brand
voice already prefers 1800+ for long-form; 1500 is the hard floor for
this specific push, not a replacement for that preference.)

**Coverage model:** plan the beats and have a framework ready now, but
don't write fake launch-day content ahead of time — house rule (and
basic honesty) is we report what's actually confirmed/discovered, not
invented. What's prepped below for Nov 19+ are *angles and structure*,
ready to fill with real information the moment it's actually out there.
Pre-launch pieces (anything dated before Nov 19) can be fully written now
since they're about real, already-known info.
